Lichess Puzzle Bot

Running

Posts the Lichess daily puzzle to Slack every day, scores your submitted line against the solution, and keeps a running leaderboard of who's solving them.

How it works

1

The daily puzzle is posted to the channel with the position shown as an image and a Submit Answer button.

2

Click the button and enter the full line in the popup, e.g. Nf3 Nc6 Bb5 - your moves and your opponent's forced replies, in order.

3

You get a DM with the result. Solve it correctly and a note is posted in the thread; the leaderboard is posted to the channel on its own schedule.

What it looks like

1. Puzzle posted
Position image + Submit Answer button.
2. Solved in thread
Correct solves get called out publicly.
3. Result by DM
Your line and score, sent privately.
This is a backend service - there's nothing else to do here. Interact with it from Slack.
